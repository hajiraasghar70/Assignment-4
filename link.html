1.Fitness

Physical fitness is a broad state of well-being defined as the body's ability to perform daily tasks, work, and recreational activities efficiently with vigor, alertness, and enough spare energy to handle unexpected emergencies.Detailed Meaning of FitnessThe concept of fitness extends far beyond simply exercising or visiting a gym. It incorporates how well your body systems—such as your heart, lungs, muscles, and metabolism—collaborate to sustain healthy living and resist chronic illness.According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), physical fitness means “the ability to carry out daily tasks with vigor and alertness without undue fatigue, and with ample energy to enjoy leisure time pursuits and respond to emergencies.”Core Components of FitnessFitness is generally measured through specific physical attributes divided into health-related and skill-related categories:Cardiorespiratory Endurance: The ability of the heart and lungs to supply oxygen-rich blood to working muscles over sustained periods (such as running or swimming).Muscular Strength: The maximum amount of force a muscle or muscle group can generate during a single contraction or heavy lift.Muscular Endurance: The capacity of a muscle to repeat contractions or maintain a static position over time without tiring.Flexibility: The full functional range of motion available around a specific joint or set of joints.Body Composition: The relative ratio of fat mass compared to lean mass (muscle, bone, and water) within the human body.

2.Walking

A "long walk" is generally defined as a continuous period of walking lasting 45 minutes to over two hours, or covering a distance of at least 6 to 10 miles (10 to 16 kilometers).Because fitness levels and environments vary, the exact threshold of what makes a walk "long" is subjective. Most people on Reddit agree that a walk reaching 45 minutes begins to feel significant, while casual fitness guidelines often classify anything past two hours or 6 miles as long-distance walking.Key ThresholdsTime-based: Sessions lasting 45 minutes to 1 hour are commonly viewed as a long daily walk, while endurance or fitness definitions often extend this to over 2 hours.Distance-based: Casual walkers often designate 6 miles (10 km) as long, whereas avid hikers usually reserve the term for routes of 10 miles or more.Health context: Studies on cardiovascular benefits contrast brief movement with sustained walks lasting 15 minutes or longer, treating anything substantially beyond standard daily movement as an extended bout.To help tailor this definition, would you like to know more about:Health benefits of long walks versus short walksTips for preparing and fueling for a long-distance walkJust let me know your preferred focus!

3.Stretching

Stretching means lengthening, widening, or extending something beyond its normal dimensions, or doing physical exercises to flex and elongate the muscles.Physical and Fitness MeaningMuscle extension: A form of physical exercise where specific muscles or limbs are deliberately flexed and elongated to boost flexibility, mobility, and range of motion.Body awakening: The natural act of extending the arms, legs, or torso after waking up or sitting for a long time.Material and Spatial MeaningPulling objects: Making a soft or elastic material (like rubber, dough, or fabric) longer or wider by pulling its edges.Spanning a distance: Spreading or extending continuously across a space, such as a road, river, or mountain range.Figurative MeaningExceeding limits: Straining something beyond normal bounds, such as "stretching" one's patience, budget, or the truth (exaggerating).Duration: Extending an event, time period, or task over a longer duration than planned.If you'd like, let me know if you are looking for information on fitness stretching routines, material science, or a specific idiom so I can provide more details.

4.Youga

Yoga means "union" or "to yoke" in Sanskrit, describing a spiritual and physical practice that brings harmony between the mind, body, and spirit.Core Meaning and PhilosophySanskrit Root: The word comes from yuj, which means to join, unite, or hitch up.Union of Self: It represents joining individual consciousness with universal consciousness or balancing the mind and body.Stilling the Mind: Ancient texts like the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali define yoga as calming the constant fluctuations and thoughts of the mind.Modern vs. Traditional PracticeTraditional View: A lifelong spiritual and mental discipline aimed at self-realization and liberation.Modern View: A popular system of physical postures (asanas), breathwork (pranayama), and meditation used for health, flexibility, and stress

5.Running

Running means moving fast on foot, operating a machine or business, or continuing without stopping.Main DefinitionsPhysical Movement: Moving quickly on foot where both feet leave the ground during the motion.Management and Operation: Managing or directing an organization, system, or business.Continuous State: Happening without stopping or over a period of time, such as a running total or a running joke.Fluid Flow: Water or other liquids moving in a stream rather than standing still
